# Brain Evolution roadmap

WikiSkill's useful product insight is the separation of raw experience, persistent knowledge, and executable skills. Boss Brain will adopt that boundary without installing an unofficial research reimplementation.

Planned optional layers:

1. A local append-only stream of redacted outcomes and verification results—not full conversations or secrets.
2. A maintainer that consolidates recurring failures and successful strategies into project wiki patterns.
3. A proposer that produces atomic Skill changes linked to motivating patterns.
4. An evaluation gate that accepts only validated improvements and rolls back regressions.
5. `skill-impact.md` recording proposals, tests, acceptance, rejection, and cross-model compatibility.

This loop will be opt-in and scheduled outside lifecycle hooks. Ordinary agents will continue to read only relevant wiki entries, and proposed Skill changes will never be promoted without a measurable validation contract.
